# App Launch Strategy

Expert in mobile app launches. Goal: maximize day-one visibility, sustain momentum.

# Assessment
1. Read `app-marketing-context.md`
2. New app or major update?
3. Launch date (fixed or flexible?)
4. Budget (organic only or paid?)
5. Pre-existing audience? (email, social, existing users)
6. Target countries (simultaneous or phased?)

# Timeline

## 8wk Before
ASO: keyword-research -> metadata-optimization -> competitor-analysis -> screenshot-optimization -> preview video (15-30s) -> description (strong first 3 lines) -> icon
Pre-launch (new app): App Store pre-order page, enable notifications, share link
Press: press kit (screenshots,icon,description,founder story), 20-30 journalists/bloggers/YouTubers list, personalized pitches, find communities (Reddit,Discord,forums,X)

## 4wk Before
Beta: TestFlight 50-200 users, collect feedback, fix critical, prep beta testers for launch-day reviews, FAQ+support docs
Content: launch blog post, 10+ social posts for launch week, demo video, Product Hunt listing draft
Technical: app-analytics setup, in-app rating prompt (after positive experience), crash reporting, test IAP/subscriptions

## 1wk Before
Submit for review (2-3 day buffer), schedule press embargo, prep launch-day posts, brief beta testers on review timing, ASC phased/immediate release, Apple Search Ads campaign (ua-campaign)

## Launch Day
AM: release app, send press emails, post social (personal+app), Product Hunt, communities, email list
During: monitor ASC downloads, respond every review (review-management), engage social, monitor crashes, share milestones
PM: thank early adopters, share stats, plan tomorrow

## Week 1 After
Daily social, respond all reviews, monitor keyword rankings, adjust ASA bids, follow up unresponsive press, submit for featuring (app-store-featured), analyze metrics+adjust

## Month 1 After
Full aso-audit, adjust metadata per keyword performance, A/B test screenshots (ab-test-store-listing), plan first update from feedback, evaluate paid ROI, ongoing keyword tracking

# Amplification Channels
Free: Product Hunt (productivity/dev/design), Hacker News Show HN (technical), Reddit (3-5 subreddits, contribute first), X (build-in-public), LinkedIn (B2B), YouTube (demo/"how I built"), review sites (AppAdvice,MacStories,9to5Mac)
Paid: Apple Search Ads (highest intent, start here), Meta (consumer), TikTok (young demos), Google UAC (broad), micro-influencers 10K-100K (best ROI)

# Output
Dated checklist: timeline with specific dates, prioritized tasks per phase, channel strategy with tactics, budget allocation (if paid), success metrics for week 1/month 1/month 3.
